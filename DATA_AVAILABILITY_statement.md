# Data availability statement

The source data and plotting metadata for all main-text and supplementary figures are provided in the accompanying repository/Zenodo archive. These files include the processed DAS-derived metrics, hydrological/displacement/image-derived plotting tables, voxel-based damage-core metrics, model-comparison tables, sensitivity-analysis metadata, and figure-level source tables required to reproduce the reported plots.

The complete raw DAS recordings are not included in the public archive because of their large data volume and site-sensitive experimental metadata. Raw DAS data are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request at `[corresponding_author_email]`, subject to data-transfer feasibility and any applicable institutional data-sharing requirements. Requests should include the requester name, affiliation, intended use, requested time windows/cases/channels, and preferred transfer method.

> Source data and plotting metadata for all figures are available at GitHub: `[GitHub URL]` and archived at Zenodo: `[Zenodo DOI]`.
